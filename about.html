About ZIPZY

Refreshing, natural, and made to enjoy anywhere.

ZIPZY

ZipZy started with a simple idea: a cold, fruity drink that actually tastes like fruit. No fuss, no complicated ingredients, just bright flavors you can enjoy any time of the day.

We love the fruits we grew up with, like calamansi, mango, and pineapple, so we made them the stars of every can. Pop it open, take a sip, and let the day feel a little lighter.

Whether you are heading to school, work, or a weekend hangout, ZipZy is small enough to bring with you and refreshing enough to make the moment better.
